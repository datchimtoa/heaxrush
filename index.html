            THE SHORT VERSION

            Get into the rhythm.

            1. Rotate

              Tap either side of the board or use ← → / A D to rotate the hexagon.

            2. Match

              Connect 3+ blocks of the same color. Matches clear across neighboring sides, too.

            3. Keep it going

              Clear again within 5 seconds to grow your chain. Its multiplier tops out at 3×. Don’t overflow the outer hexagon.

            3 blocks = 10 · 4 = 18 · 5 = 30
            Each block above 5 adds 12 base points.
            Desktop: hold ↓ / S to rush · Space / P to pause
